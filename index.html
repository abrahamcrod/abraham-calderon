About Me

Welcome! I am an Operations Research and Infrastructure Analytics professional with 5 years of international experience spanning transport modeling, infrastructure finance, and quantitative optimization. I am currently pursuing my MSc in Operations Research & Analytics at The London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE).

My core technical expertise bridges mathematical programming (AMPL, CPLEX, HiGHS), statistical computing (R, Python), and spatial analytics (QGIS). Previously, as a Senior Consultant at Steer in London, I led transport economics, traffic forecasting, and multi-lateral e-mobility advisory mandates across Europe, Central Asia, and Latin America.

Focus Areas

My professional and academic interests center on applying advanced analytics to complex real-world systems:

  • Deterministic and stochastic mathematical optimization models.
  • Transport economics, infrastructure finance, and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) frameworks.
  • Data-driven decision support for sustainable mobility and public infrastructure development.
